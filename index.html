Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

ArabiSabak - Learn Gulf Arabic ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

ArabiSabak is designed with privacy in mind. The App operates primarily offline and collects minimal data:

  • Learning Progress: Your lesson progress, bookmarks, and favorite words are stored locally on your device only.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as theme preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising Identifier: The App displays banner advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To serve those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK accesses your device's advertising ID and sends it to Google. See section 4 below.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Text-to-Speech

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech (TTS) engine to pronounce Arabic words and phrases. Audio is processed locally on your device. No voice data is sent to external servers.

3. Data Storage

All of your learning data (progress, favorites, settings) is stored locally on your device using shared preferences. We operate no servers of our own, and none of this data is transmitted to us or stored by us.

The one exception is advertising: the Google AdMob SDK built into the App sends your device's advertising ID and related technical information to Google in order to request and display ads. That data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: The App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID and technical data such as device type, IP address, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and to detect invalid traffic. This data is collected and shared by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: The App may download fonts from Google Fonts to display Arabic text. Google's privacy policy applies to this service.
  • Google Play Store: The App is distributed through Google Play, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

ArabiSabak is intended for users aged 18 and over, and is listed on Google Play with that target age group. The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the App displays third-party advertising that relies on an advertising identifier, it is not designed or intended for use by children.

If you believe a child has used the App, you can clear the App's data from your device settings and reset the device's advertising ID as described in section 7.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security settings. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device software up to date.

7. Your Rights

You can delete all App data at any time by:

  • Clearing the App's data through your device settings
  • Uninstalling the App

You can also control the advertising ID used for ads from your device: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID and to opt out of ad personalisation. On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
